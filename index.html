Privacy, Account Deletion & User Support

Privacy Policy

ReBuy is a marketplace app where users can buy and sell used items.

The app may process information such as your name, email address, phone number, profile photo, listing photos and descriptions, approximate or precise location (when permitted), favorites, and in-app chat messages to provide its features.

ReBuy uses Firebase services for app functions such as account authentication and storing app data. Location and advertising services may also process information when those features are enabled.

We use information to operate the app, display listings, support communication between users, maintain accounts, and help protect the service. Do not share passwords or sensitive personal information in public listings or chats.

To ask about your information or privacy, email rebuysupport@gmail.com.

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy should be reviewed and updated to accurately reflect all services, data practices, retention periods, and legal requirements applicable to ReBuy.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your ReBuy account by emailing rebuysupport@gmail.com with the subject ReBuy Account Deletion Request.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your account, if possible. We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership. Never send your password or verification code.

We will review the request and explain the next steps. Account deletion and associated data removal must be completed in accordance with our applicable retention and legal obligations.

Data Deletion Without Account Deletion

If you want to request deletion of specific information without deleting your entire ReBuy account, email rebuysupport@gmail.com with the subject ReBuy Data Deletion Request.

Tell us which information you want reviewed. We will assess your request and explain what can be deleted while keeping your account active.
